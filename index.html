Landlord Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Landlord is a rental marketplace that helps people discover accommodation and allows property owners and agents to submit rental listings. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Landlord application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Landlord may handle:

  • Account and authentication information, such as your email address and information you provide with your account.
  • Property listing details, including property type, prices, location, descriptions, rental terms and facilities.
  • Photos and videos you choose to upload with a property listing.
  • Phone numbers or other contact details you provide for enquiries.
  • Properties you save to your account.
  • Property location details and device location information if you choose to use a feature that requests it.
  • Technical information processed by service providers to operate, maintain and secure their services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Display, manage and review property listings.
  • Show listing photos, videos, prices, locations and rental terms.
  • Allow users to save properties and make enquiries.
  • Operate, maintain, secure and improve the application.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate misuse.

4. Public Property Listings

Information in a listing may become visible to other users when the listing is approved for publication. This may include descriptions, rental prices, location details, photos, videos and contact information provided for enquiries. Do not include confidential information in a public listing unless you intend it to be public.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

Landlord uses Supabase for backend services, which may include authentication, database and file storage. Supabase and relevant infrastructure providers may process information to provide these services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address security, fraud, abuse or protection of users and others. Landlord does not sell personal information to third parties for their own advertising purposes.

6. Storage and Security

Information is stored and processed using the services configured for Landlord. Measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration are used as appropriate. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, manage accounts and listings, address security needs and comply with legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain information where legally required or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information you submit in listings and whether to use optional device features that request permissions. Revoking a permission may limit the related feature. Information already submitted may require a separate deletion request.

9. Children's Privacy

Landlord is a rental marketplace and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards will be used where required.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application or its data practices change. The latest version and update date will be published on this webpage.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact Landlord at nixona2024@gmail.com.
